## Army Rules

##### Waaaaagh!


While your Warlord is on the battlefield (but not embarked in a <span class="keyword" data-term="transport" data-type="keyword">Transport</span>), each <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> unit that can draw line of sight to your Warlord gain abilities based on the actions of your Warlord, as follows:
- If your Warlord made a Ranged attack this Battle Round as part of a <span class="keyword" data-term="shoot" data-type="action">Shoot</span> Action, <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> units that have not yet been activated improve the Ballistic Skill characteristic of models in the unit by 1.
- If your Warlord successfully Charged an enemy unit this Battle Round as part of a <span class="keyword" data-term="charge-and-fight" data-type="action">Charge and Fight</span> Action, <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> units that have not yet been activated increase their Weapons Skill characteristic by 1.
- If your Warlord made a Melee attack this Battle Round as part of a <span class="keyword" data-term="charge-and" data-type="action">Charge and</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="fight" data-type="action">Fight </span>Action, <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> units that have not yet been activated score Critical Hits for Melee attacks on a 5+ instead of a 6+.

If your Warlord is destroyed, all of the above abilities become permanently activated for all <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> units in your army for the remainder of the battle.


##### Nob Taktiks


Each <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> unit that includes a Nob can use this ability while that unit (and its Nob) are on the battlefield, including while embarked in a <span class="keyword" data-term="transport" data-type="keyword">Transport</span>. To do so, when the unit is Activated, roll a D6, subtracting 1 if the unit is currently Battle Shocked. On a 4+, select 1 of the following Nob Taktiks to be in effect for the unit until the end of the Battle Round:


<h6 id="more-dakka">More Dakka!</h6>

- Increase the Attacks characteristic of Ranged weapons by 1 and gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="sustained-hits-1" data-type="keyword">Sustained Hits 1</span> ability (or increase an existing <span class="keyword" data-term="sustained-hits-x" data-type="keyword">Sustained Hits [X]</span> ability 1)
- Increase the Armour Piercing characteristic of Ranged weapons in the unit by 1 if the entire unit targets a single enemy unit.
- Reduce the Movement characteristic of models in the unit by 1"
- Reduce the Initiative characteristic of models in the unit by 1

<h6 id="krump-em">Krump 'Em!</h6>

- Increase the Movement characteristic of models in the unit by 1"
- The unit scores Critical Hits on a 5+ when making Melee attacks.
- Melee weapons equipped by the unit gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="sustained-hits-1" data-type="keyword">Sustained Hits 1</span> ability, or increase [X] by 1 if it already possesses the <span class="keyword" data-term="sustained-hits-x" data-type="keyword">Sustained Hits [X]</span> ability.

<h6 id="straightn-out">Straightn' Out!</h6>


This Taktik can only be selected by a unit that is currently Battle Shocked. When selected, destroy 1 model from the unit. Once the model is removed, the unit Rallies and increases its Leadership characteristic by 1 until the end of your next Command Phase.


##### The Beast Snaggas Clan


When a <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas</span> unit makes an attack against an enemy unit with the <span class="keyword" data-term="beast" data-type="keyword">Beast</span> or <span class="keyword" data-term="monster" data-type="keyword">Monster</span> keyword, increase the Strength characteristic of that attack by 1. If the attack scores a Critical Wound, increase the Damage characteristic of that attack by 1 as well.


##### Ramshakkle 'n Scrapbuilt


<span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="vehicle" data-type="keyword">Vehicle</span> units with the <span class="keyword" data-term="deadly-demise-x" data-type="keyword">Deadly Demise [X]</span> ability do not trigger that ability normally. Instead, each time an <span class="keyword" data-term="ork" data-type="keyword">Ork</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="vehicle" data-type="keyword">Vehicle</span> with the <span class="keyword" data-term="deadly-demise-x" data-type="keyword">Deadly Demise [X]</span> ability is destroyed, roll a D6, on a 4+, the <span class="keyword" data-term="deadly-demise-x" data-type="keyword">Deadly Demise [X]</span> ability is triggered.


## Detachment Traits

Your Ork army can select 1 of the following Detachment Traits. These detachment traits offer Force Organization and army structure changes to represent various infamous Ork formations and their warfare styles on the tabletop. 


<h6 id="green-tide">Green Tide</h6>


Each time a <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> unit from your army is destroyed, roll a D6. On a roll of a 4 or 5, up to half of the models in the unit can be placed into Strategic Reserves and may be Activated in the next Battle Round. On a roll of a 6, the whole unit can be placed into Strategic Reserves and may be Activated in the next Battle Round.


<h6 id="speed-freeks">Speed Freeks</h6>

- Bikerz gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> keyword
- Boyz lose the <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> keyword
- Any <span class="keyword" data-term="orks-infantry" data-type="keyword">Orks Infantry</span> units in your army that do not have the <span class="keyword" data-term="mounted" data-type="keyword">Mounted</span> or <span class="keyword" data-term="jump-pack" data-type="keyword">Jump Pack</span> keywords must start the battle embarked in a Transport.
- Your army cannot include any <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas </span>units

<h6 id="snaggin-raid">Snaggin' Raid</h6>


The following Force Organization changes must be adhered to:
- Beast Snaggas Boyz gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> keyword
- Boyz lose the <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> keyword
- Only units with the <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas</span> keyword can be included in your army

Snaggin' Raid can earn Victory Points by capturing enemy models so that they can be brought back to their camps to be used in bloodsports and, eventually, as trophies. This is achieved by successfully hitting enemy units with <span class="keyword" data-term="harpoon" data-type="keyword">Harpoon</span> attacks and then dragging enemy models hit by those attacks off of the battlefield with your Beast Snaggas units by Moving those units towards any battlefield edge until the whole unit can be moved off of that battlefield edge. The victory points for completing this raiding activity are described here.

- For every enemy <span class="keyword" data-term="beast" data-type="keyword">Beast</span> model that is dragged off of the battlefield by a <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas</span> unit that made an attack with a <span class="keyword" data-term="harpoon" data-type="keyword">Harpoon</span> weapon, gain 3 Victory Points.
- For every enemy <span class="keyword" data-term="monster" data-type="keyword">Monster</span> or <span class="keyword" data-term="vehicle" data-type="keyword">Vehicle</span> model that is dragged off of the battlefield by a <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas</span> unit that made an attack with a <span class="keyword" data-term="harpoon" data-type="keyword">Harpoon</span> weapon, gain 8 Victory Points.
- For every enemy <span class="keyword" data-term="character" data-type="keyword">Character</span> that is dragged off of the battlefield by a <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas</span> unit that made an attack with a <span class="keyword" data-term="harpoon" data-type="keyword">Harpoon</span> weapon, gain an additional 5 Victory Points.
- Gain 2 Victory points for each <span class="keyword" data-term="beast-snaggas" data-type="keyword">Beast Snaggas</span> unit that is removed from the battlefield while dragging an enemy unit.
