## Army Rules

##### Necrodermis


During your Command Phase, a <span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> unit that lost Wounds (or models) during the previous Battle Round can use this ability. To do so, roll a D6 and add D3+1 Wounds back to the unit. Wounds can be returned in the form of returned models that were destroyed, or Wounds returned to models that do not have their full Wounds, but the total number of Wounds returned cannot exceed value established by the D6 roll.

<span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> units can never use this ability if they have been fully destroyed. A fully destroyed unit is one where all models from the unit - excluding <span class="keyword" data-term="character" data-type="keyword">Character</span> units that are leading, or have joined the unit - have been reduced to 0 Wounds and removed from the battlefield. 


##### Command Protocols


<span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> units being led by a <span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="noble" data-type="keyword">Noble</span> unit are considered to have the same Activation Points characteristic as that <span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="noble" data-type="keyword">Noble</span>.

Additionally, each time a <span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="noble" data-type="keyword">Noble</span> unit leading a <span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> unit is Activated, that unit can select 1 other friendly <span class="keyword" data-term="necron" data-type="keyword">Necron</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> unit that they are not leading to be activated simultaneously with their own unit. When they do so, each unit must complete the same Actions in the same order. When doing so, only the lowest applicable Activation Points characteristic among the units selected can be spent.

<h6 id="standard-wargear">Standard Wargear</h6>


None.


<h6 id="abilities">Abilities</h6>


**Self-Destruct Protocol:** Each time a model from this unit is in base-to-base contact with an enemy unit, you can choose to use this ability instead of making a Melee attack. To do so, select the enemy as the subject of this attack and roll a D6. On a 1, nothing occurs and the model is destroyed. On a 2 to 4, the enemy unit suffers D3 Mortal Wounds, on a 5 or 6, the enemy unit suffers D6 Mortal Wounds. Models that use this ability are permanently destroyed and cannot use the Necrodermis ability.
