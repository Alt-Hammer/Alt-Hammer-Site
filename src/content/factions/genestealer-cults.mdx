## Army Rules

##### Cult Ambush


Every <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="infantry" data-type="keyword">Infantry </span>unit ignores the limitations on the number of units that can be placed in Strategic Reserves before the start of the battle. Instead, any and all <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults-infantry" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults Infantry </span>units in your army can be placed into Strategic Reserves. However, at least 2 <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="infantry" data-type="keyword">Infantry</span> units must <span class="keyword" data-term="enter-the-battlefield-from-strategic-reserves" data-type="action">Enter the Battlefield from Strategic Reserves</span> during each Battle Round.

Additionally, up to 3 <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="vehicle" data-type="keyword">Vehicle</span> or <span class="keyword" data-term="mounted" data-type="keyword">Mounted</span> units can always be placed into Strategic Reserves for every 1,000 points in battle size.


##### The Time is Nigh


Every <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults-infantry" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults Infantry </span>unit that uses the <span class="keyword" data-term="enter-the-battlefield-from-strategic-reserves" data-type="action">Enter the Battlefield from Strategic Reserves</span> Action via the <span class="keyword" data-term="deep-strike" data-type="keyword">Deep Strike</span> ability must adhere to the following rules for deploying to the battlefield. Instead units who <span class="keyword" data-term="enter-the-battlefield-from-strategic-reserves" data-type="action">Enter the Battlefield from Strategic Reserves</span> Action via the <span class="keyword" data-term="deep-strike" data-type="keyword">Deep Strike</span> ability must be placed such that every model is wholly or partially within 3" of a Terrain Feature. Additionally, units must be placed on the battlefield such that no model is within the following distances of any enemy models: 
- 12" in Battle Round 1
- 6" in Battle Round 2
- 3" in Battle Round 3 and onwards (if playing a custom game that lasts for longer than Countermarch's typical 3 Battle Rounds).

##### Return to the Shadows


<span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults-infantry" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults Infantry </span>units have access to the <span class="keyword" data-term="return-to-the-shadows" data-type="action">Return to the Shadows</span> Action.


##### Broodcoven 


Up to 3 <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="character" data-type="keyword">Character</span> units can join a unit being led by a Primus.


##### Brood Brothers


Up to 25% of the points value of your <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults</span> army can be comprised of the following <span class="keyword" data-term="astra-militarum" data-type="keyword">Astra Militarum</span> units:

- **Characters**
- Senior Officer
- Commissar
- Ogryn Bodyguard

**Battleline**
- Infantry Section

**Infantry**
- Command Squad
- Special Weapons Section

**Dedicated Transports**
- Chimera
- Taurox

**Walkers**
- Sentinels

**Vehicles**
- Light Artillery Teams
- Heavy Artillery Team
- Onager Self Propelled Artillery
- Chimera Self Propelled Artillery
- Hellhound Light Assault Tank
- Leman Russ Battle Tank
- Rogal Dorn Heavy Battle Tank
- Baneblade Super-Heavy Battle Tank
- Stormlord Super-Heavy Assault Transport
- Shadowsword Super-Heavy Tank Hunter

When including these units, they gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="genestealer-cults" data-type="keyword">Genestealer Cults</span> keyword, but do not benefit from this Genestealer Cults Army Rules, and cannot make selections from Astra Militarum Detachment Traits. However, you gain the ability to use the Astra Militarum's **Voice of Command** and **Regimental Coordination** Army Rules with these units.

<h6 id="ridgehauler-trailers">Ridgehauler Trailers</h6>


Each Ridgehauler Trailer in this unit must start the battle being Towed. The first Ridgehauler Trailer added to the unit must always be physically connected to the Ridgehauler (or Ironcrawler) model towing it by a trailer hitch, and second Ridgehauler Trailer must be connected to the first Ridgehauler Trailer via a similar trailer hitch. 

Each Ridgehauler Trailer model's characteristics are separate from the model towing it and can always be targeted separately from the Ridgehauler (or Ironcrawler) as if it was its own unit. 

When the Ridgehauler (or Ironcrawler) model is itself destroyed, any Ridgehauler Trailers being towed by that model can only use the <span class="keyword" data-term="shoot" data-type="action">Shoot</span> Action in subsequent Activations. Should the first Ridgehauler Trailer be destroyed before the second, the second then becomes Detached from the Ridgehauler (or Ironcrawler) model that was previously Towing it, and counts as a separate unit. However, a player can choose to re-connect the remaining Ridgehauler Trailer by completing a <span class="keyword" data-term="move" data-type="action">Move</span> Action with the Ridgehauler (or Ironcrawler) that ends with the rear quadrant of the model within 1" of the Ridgehauler Trailer. At the start of the next Battle Round, the Ridgehauler Trailer is considered to be Towed again, and the units are considered 1 unit once more.
