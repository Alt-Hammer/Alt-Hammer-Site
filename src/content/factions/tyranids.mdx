## Army Rules

##### Synapse Control


Several Tyranids units possess the <span class="keyword" data-term="synapse-x" data-type="keyword">Synapse [X]"</span> keyword, allowing these units to control and enhance the resilience and use of lesser bioforms more common to a Tyranids army. The <span class="keyword" data-term="synapse-x" data-type="keyword">Synapse [X]"</span> keyword confers the following ability:


<h6 id="synapse-x">Synapse [X]"</h6>


This unit provides the following benefits to all <span class="keyword" data-term="tyranids" data-type="keyword">Tyranids</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="beast" data-type="keyword">Beast</span> units with at least one model within [X]" of this unit:
- Increase the number of Activation Points available to the unit by 1
- Gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="fearless" data-type="keyword">Fearless</span> ability
- Ignore the requirement to take a Breach Test when entering a terrain feature with the <span class="keyword" data-term="building" data-type="keyword">Building</span> keyword

##### Broodswarm


When it is your turn to Activate a unit, you can declare that you will use the Broodswarm ability. To do so, select 1 of the following units to be Activated: 
- Termagaunts
- Hormagaunts
- Gargoyles

If that unit is under Synapse Control, you can then select up to 2 more of the same unit in your army that are also under Synapse Control and activate all 3 of those units simultaneously. When you do so, all 3 units must all complete the same Actions in the same order. 

**Example Scenario:** The player declares they are using the Broodswarm ability as part of their Activation and selects 1 unit of Hormagaunts that is under Synapse Control by a nearby friendly Warriors unit. This condition allows the player to select 2 other Hormagaunts units in Synapse Control range of other Synapse [X]" units. The player now has 3 Hormagaunts units can be activated Simultaneously as part of this Activation. They then elect to complete a Move Action, and all 3 units Move. This is followed by a Charge and Fight Action, and all 3 units select enemy units to Charge and Fight. During this process, 1 of the Hormagaunt units does not successfully end its Charge move in Base-to-Base Contact with any enemy models, and thus that unit's Activation is effectively over. However, the other 2 units have successful Charge moves and process to complete their Fights as part of that Charge and Fight Action. Once the Fights are completed from these 2 units, neither unit have any remaining eligible Actions that can be made with each unit's remaining Activation Point, and thus the Broodswarm Activation is finished.


##### Shadow in the Warp


Enemy <span class="keyword" data-term="psykers" data-type="keyword">Psykers</span> must take a Battle Shock Test at the start of their Activation when they are wholly within [X]" of at least 1 Tyranid unit with the <span class="keyword" data-term="synapse-x" data-type="keyword">Synapse [X]"</span> ability. If the Battle Shock Test is failed, that enemy <span class="keyword" data-term="psyker" data-type="keyword">Psyker</span> is Battle Shocked.


## Detachment Traits

Your Tyranid army can select 1 of the following Detachment Traits. 


<h6 id="endless-swarm">Endless Swarm</h6>

- Each time a <span class="keyword" data-term="tyranid-battleline-beast" data-type="keyword">Tyranid Battleline Beast</span> unit from your army is Activated, if that unit is within range of a unit with the Synapse [X]" ability, you can immediately activate 1 other Battleline unit of the same type on the battlefield. Both of these units count as a single Activation, but must complete the same Actions at the same time.
- Each time a <span class="keyword" data-term="tyranid-battleline-beast" data-type="keyword">Tyranid Battleline Beast</span> model from your army is destroyed, roll 1 D6. On a 5+ place that model into a group off of the table. This is your next Endless Swarm Wave. At the start of the next Battle Round, organize the models in your Endless Swarm Wave into units of matching models - 1 unit per model type (ie, 1 unit of Termagaunts, 1 unit of Hormagaunts), which are then considered to be in Strategic Reserves. These units must then be Activated (following the standard rules for activating units in Strategic Reserves) and must be placed anywhere on the battlefield so that it is wholly within 6" of any Battlefield Edge in your Deployment Zone.

<h6 id="vanguard-invaders">Vanguard Invaders</h6>

The following units lose the <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> keyword: Termagaunts, Hormagaunts, Warriors


<h6 id="crusher-stampede">Crusher Stampede</h6>

## Wargear Upgrades

<h6 id="adrenal-glands">Adrenal Glands<span class="heading-value">Varies (see below)</span></h6>


The bearer increases its Initiative characteristic by 2.

The points cost for this upgrade is as follows:

5 points per model for models with a Toughness characteristic of 3 or lower

15 points per model for models with a Toughness characteristic of 4 or higher


<h6 id="hardened-carapace">Hardened Carapace<span class="heading-value">Varies (see below)</span></h6>


The bearer increases its Wounds characteristic by 3.

The points cost for this upgrade is as follows:

10 points per model for models with a Toughness characteristic of 6 or lower

20 points per model for models with a Toughness characteristic of 7 or higher


<h6 id="scything-wings">Scything Wings<span class="heading-value">30 Points</span></h6>

- Increase Movement characteristic to 12"
- Gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="fly" data-type="keyword">Fly</span> keyword
- Gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="deep-strike" data-type="keyword">Deep Strike</span> ability 
- Includes <span class="weapon-ref" data-weapon="scything-wings">Scything Wings</span> Melee weapon (you do not need to pay the points cost for this weapon as part of this Wargear upgrade)

<h6 id="serpentine-bioform">Serpentine Bioform<span class="heading-value">15 Points</span></h6>

- Cannot be equipped by models equipped with <span class="wargear-ref" data-wargear="scything-wings">Scything Wings</span>
- Increase Movement characteristic to 9"
- Gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="deep-strike" data-type="keyword">Deep Strike</span> ability

<h6 id="neuroloids">Neuroloids<span class="heading-value">5 Points</span></h6>


For each Neuroloid included with this unit, extend the Synapse Range of this unit by 6".


<h6 id="feeder-tendrils">Feeder Tendrils<span class="heading-value">8 Points</span></h6>


Each time this model destroys an enemy <span class="keyword" data-term="infantry" data-type="keyword">Infantry</span> <span class="keyword" data-term="character" data-type="keyword">Character</span> model with a Melee attack, increase its current number of Wounds by D3.


<h6 id="warp-field">Warp Field<span class="heading-value">15 Points</span></h6>


Models equipped with a Warp Field gain the <span class="keyword" data-term="invulnerable-save-5" data-type="keyword">Invulnerable Save 5+</span> ability. Additionally, once per Battle, models equipped with a Warp Field can attempt to enhance their Warp Field. To do so, take a Psychic Test. If the test is passed, the bearer gains the <span class="keyword" data-term="invulnerable-save-4" data-type="keyword">Invulnerable Save 4+</span> ability until the end of the Battle Round.


<h6 id="spore-launcher">Spore Launcher<span class="heading-value">15 Points</span></h6>


When equipped with a Spore Launcher, this model can make a Ranged attack with that weapon by placing D3 <span class="weapon-ref" data-weapon="spore">Spore</span> models at least 12" and up to 90" away from this model. Those <span class="weapon-ref" data-weapon="spore">Spore</span> models then becomes their own Spores unit(s) that can be activated as described under the Spores unit profile. If you choose to place any of these <span class="weapon-ref" data-weapon="spore">Spore</span> models within 6" horizontally and/or diagonally of each other, those Spore models become a single Spores unit.


<h6 id="twin-linked">Twin-linked<span class="heading-value">Varies (see below)</span></h6>


Weapon gains the <span class="keyword" data-term="twin-linked" data-type="keyword">Twin-linked</span> ability. 

This cost of this wargear upgrade is determined as follows:
- For weapons with a Strength characteristic of 9 or more, add the original points cost of the weapon. This includes weapons that have 1 or more attack profiles where 1 of those attack profiles has a Strength characteristic of 9 or more 
- For weapons with a Strength characteristic of 8 or less, add half of the original points cost of the weapon, rounding up.
