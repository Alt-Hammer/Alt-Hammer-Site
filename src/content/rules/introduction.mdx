Countermarch is a ruleset designed to provide an alternative style of Warhammer 40,000 gameplay using alternating unit activations. 

The intent with Countermarch is to bring Warhammer 40,000's intricate rules designs that are familiar to many Warhammer 40,000 players into more tactical gameplay where each player alternates between individual unit activations, effectively completing their turns - and full battle rounds - simultaneously. 

This site outlines the Core Rules that constitute Countermarch gameplay. It covers:
- **Battle Round Structure:** how each of the 3 steps in a Battle Round are conducted.
- **Activations & Activation Points:** the core mechanics for unit activations - what they can do - and how these govern the interactions between armies during the game.
- **General Gameplay Rules:** including force organization rules, minimum army requirements, unit coherency, and rules for building your army.
- **How to Make Attacks:** the process for making attacks, with distinct rules for Ranged and Melee combat, including how combat is resolved.
- **Battle Shock:** the process and effects of Battle Shock, Countermarch's morale system.
- **Strategic Reserves:** the rules for placing units in Strategic Reserves, including how and when these units can enter the battlefield and any restrictions on their deployment. 
- **Primary Mission Objectives:** the rules for determining, placing, and controlling mission Primary Objective Markers, as well as how Victory Points are awarded for controlling these objectives, and how Objective Control values determine which player controls a Primary Objective Marker.
- **Command Points & Stratagems:** an Optional game feature that offers players some familiar situational boosts.
- **Optional Game Features:** additional Mission Rules and Secondary Objectives that can be included to add variety and strategic depth to the game.

### The Fundamental Design Concepts of Countermarch


Countermarch has been designed with these fundamental design concepts:
- **Additive Points** - Countermarch exclusively uses Points to determine battle sizes, and every model and piece of wargear is an additive cost. Want to replace a standard weapon with a special weapon option? Subtract the standard weapon's points costs and add the special weapon's points costs instead. The List Builder on this site does all the math for you. 
- **Highly customizable - and fewer - units to build an army with** - Rather than having separate unit profiles for a variety of fixed combinations of equipment and abilities for a single unit type, you instead work with a much more limited number of individual units, and many of them allow deep customization to create wargear loadouts that can be familiar and iconic, or much less so. Optionality is the name of the game, and you can keep it simple or make some very unique and flavourful setups that suit your style.
- **Attacking enemies is a comparison of skills** - Weapons Skill and Ballistics Skill are fixed values for models in your army, and you compare them against those of the foe you are facing to see how well you can Hit. This means the Hit rolls you make can vary a lot. A mighty Intercessor can sweep away rank-and-file Cultists effortlessly, but they will have a much harder time Hitting the Daemon Prince behind them.
- **High impact attacks against optimal targets** - lethality in Countermarch is optimized for intended targets. Clearing chaff? Weapons best suited for that have loads of attacks, but low strength and low damage. Going for big targets? High strength, high damage, but very few attacks - so make them count! Lethality quickly loses efficiency when you make attacks against sub-optimal targets. Countermarch also has instant death and lethality 'ceilings' where super low strength attacks only have a chance at inflicting damage if you get very *very* lucky. 
- **Virtually all special rules and abilities are universal** - Outside of Faction Rules and unique abilities (mostly on Characters and some unique units),** **there are very few unit-specific special rules. Once you get familiar with Countermarch's universal Keywords and Abilities, you'll know most of the special rules and abilities in the game. Mastering these is just about knowing which rules and abilities are available to a given army, model or unit, which is done via Keywords. This site's Faction Rules, List Builder and Army Roster (your exported list) makes referencing these rules a breeze with tooltips that cite specific rules content related to various Keywords, Weapons, Wargear Upgrades, and more.
- **Re-rolls are virtually eliminated** - with one exception, there is no rule in Countermarch that provides you the ability to re-roll a D6. And the one exception is optional (it's a Stratagem).
