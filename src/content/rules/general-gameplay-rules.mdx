## Faction Rules


Every Countermarch Faction falls under one of the following overarching Faction Groups: 

Imperium

Chaos

Aeldari

The Great Devourer

Broader Xenos Species

Each Faction within these Groups offers:
- **Faction Rules:** one or more unique abilities that units from that Faction benefit from when your Army is built from that Faction. You will note that many Faction Rules allow a Faction to be taken as an additional Faction if your Warlord is from another allied Faction, or allow you to include units from multiple allied Factions in some limited capacity.
- **Detachment Traits:** an additional list of traits that allow you to customize your Detachment thematically, offering play style and detachment force composition flexibility within a Faction's available units. Detachment Traits are sometimes selected using Detachment Points, and limitations on which Detachment Traits can be taken alongside others are prevalent within each Faction's list of Detachment Traits.
- **Wargear Upgrades:** Many units and models within a Faction have access to various forms of Wargear that they can be equipped with, some unique and others less so. The points cost and rules for these upgrades are detailed for each Faction under a Wargear Upgrades section. Units that can make selections  from these Wargear Upgrades detail what those selections are under a 'Wargear Options' section of their Unit Profile.
- **Unit Profiles:** The units available to each Faction, the models that comprise that unit and their characteristics, and unit sizes. Additional details on what is covered in Unit Profiles is detailed below under Unit Rules. 

In some cases, your army can include units from 1 or more Faction from within an Overarching Faction. Doing so often includes specific limitations on the number of units, points values, Faction-specific keywords, etc. The rules for taking advantage of this are detailed under Army Rules within a Faction that provides this option for constructing your army.


## Force Organization


The Faction you play a game of Countermarch with is comprised of Units. There is no limit on the total number of Units your force can include. However, your army's force organization must abide by the following limitations applied to the number of individual units that your force can include: 

Your army must include at least 1 <span class="keyword" data-term="leader" data-type="keyword">Leader</span> unit. 

1 <span class="keyword" data-term="leader" data-type="keyword">Leader</span> unit in your army must be designated as your Warlord. Note that some Factions have <span class="keyword" data-term="leader" data-type="keyword">Leader</span> units that must be your Warlord if you take them, as described in their Unit Profile under a Force Organization section.

You can include a maximum of 1 of the same unit in your army for every 500 points, unless that unit:
- Possesses the <span class="keyword" data-term="battleline" data-type="keyword">Battleline</span> keyword, in which case you can take up to 2x of the unit for every 500 points
- Possesses the <span class="keyword" data-term="character" data-type="keyword">Character</span> keyword, in which case you can take up to 1x of the unit for every 1,000 points
- Possesses the <span class="keyword" data-term="titanic" data-type="keyword">Titanic</span> keyword, in which case you can only include such units in battles that are 1,500 points or larger. These units are also limited to 1x of the unit for every 1,000 points in battle size.
- Possesses the Epic Hero keyword, in which case you can only ever include 1 such unit in your army.
- Is from the **Dedicated Transports** unit category, in which case you can take 1x such unit for every <span class="keyword" data-term="infantry" data-type="keyword">Infantry</span> unit in your army that is eligible to be embarked in a <span class="keyword" data-term="transport" data-type="keyword">Transport</span>.
- Has other Unit-specific conditions on the number that can be included in your army which overwrite any of the above general force organization rules. Such rules are detailed in the Unit Profile under a Force Organization section.

## Unit Coherency


Units in Countermarch that have more than 1 model must maintain Unit Coherency - ie, how close models in the unit have to be positioned to one another - on the battlefield at all times. Unit Coherency is measured in Inches and varies based on the number of models in the unit, as detailed here: 
- Every model in a unit with 2 to 10 models must remain wholly within 8" of every other model in the unit 
- Every model in a unit with 11 to 20 models must remain wholly within 10" of every other model in the unit
- Every model in a unit with 21 to 30 models must remain wholly within 12" of every other model in the unit
- Every model in a unit with 31 models or more must remain wholly within 14" of every other model in the unit

These coherency values apply to both horizontal and vertical distances.

Models in units that cannot maintain Unit Coherency for any given reason must be removed as casualties.


## Unit Rules


The Detachment you build for a Faction to suit a battle's game size is done by selecting Units. Every unit has a Unit Profile that outlines the rules for constructing that unit:
- **Model Characteristics** for the model(s) that can be included as part of that unit.
- **Unit Composition** in terms of number of models that can be taken in that unit.
- **Standard wargear** that models in the unit are equipped with by default. In some instances, this section details a required *selection* between a list of options that must be made. Remember that all points in Countermarch are additive, so Wargear costs are additional to any unit/model costs applicable to that unit, the values for which are contained in the Weapon Data Tables.
- **Special abilities** that the unit possesses and can use during a battle. 
- **Wargear options** that the unit has access to, 
- **Leader abilities**, if applicable, that identifies units that the unit can be attached to as a Leader.
- **Force Organization considerations** that supersede standard Countermarch's standard Force Organization rules. For example, some units are limited to 1 per Detachment. In some cases, units are required to be your Warlord if your army includes that unit.

**Wargear Options** available to a unit often have limitations that are explained through specific language used in the Unit Profile. Examples of how to interpret that language are provided here:
- *"Any model in the unit can..."* means any number of models in the unit can select options described. This does not mean *every* model in the unit has to select the upgrade.
- *"Every model in the unit can..."* means the whole unit - every model - must select the upgrade described.
- *"Up to [x] models can..."* means no more than the number of models identified by [x] can choose the upgrade described.
- *"For every [y] models in the unit, [x] model(s) can"* is similar to above, but with the added constraint that the upgrade can only be taken when the total unit model count is met. For example, if a unit's total model count is 10, and the upgrade states that *"for every 5 models in the unit, 1 model can..."* then 2 models can take the upgrade; 1 for every 5 models in the unit of 10 models. But if that same unit's total model count is only 7, then only 1 model could take that upgrade.
